# S0 hypothesis

With a frozen `paraphrase-mpnet-base-v2` representation and identical train-only
compression, data splits, seeds, and selection budgets, a compact QAtelier head
will reproduce the qualitative low-data behavior of a published-style frozen
sentence-encoder quantum-head setup. The result is falsified for calibration
purposes if the implementation cannot execute the declared controls or if an
observed discrepancy cannot be explained by the declared resource and protocol
differences.

This hypothesis does not predict quantum superiority and does not authorize
physical hardware execution.
